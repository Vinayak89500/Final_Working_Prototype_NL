About Claude Compass

What is Claude Compass?

Claude Compass is an innovative AI-Native Decision Intelligence platform. As AI models become more capable, choosing how to interact with them becomes more complex. Should you use a simple prompt, string multiple prompts together in a workflow, or deploy an autonomous agent? Claude Compass answers this question.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between human intent and AI execution. By analyzing the logic, complexity, and desired output of any given task, we route users to the most cost-effective and highly capable execution mode. This saves time, reduces token costs, and improves overall AI output quality.

How it Works

Users input a natural language description of their task. Our backend routing system processes the intent, breaks it down into logical steps, and generates a capability scorecard. This scorecard compares different execution methodologies and provides a definitive recommendation.
